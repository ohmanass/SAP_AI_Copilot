SAP SD - SALES ORDER MANAGEMENT

1. Overview

SAP Sales and Distribution (SD) is a module used to manage the order-to-cash process: customer inquiries and quotations, sales orders, outbound deliveries, billing and the handover to financial accounting.

2. Order-to-Cash Process

The sales process generally follows these steps:

1. Create an inquiry or a quotation (optional).
2. Create a sales order.
3. Check availability and confirm delivery dates.
4. Create the outbound delivery.
5. Pick, pack and post the goods issue.
6. Create the billing document (customer invoice).
7. Post the incoming payment in financial accounting.

3. Sales Order Structure

A sales order is a contract between a company and a customer for the delivery of goods or services at agreed prices, quantities and dates.

In SAP, sales orders are represented by these main database tables:

- VBAK: stores sales document header information.
- VBAP: stores sales document item information.
- VBEP: stores schedule lines (delivery dates and confirmed quantities).
- VBPA: stores the business partners of the document.

4. VBAK Table

VBAK contains general information about a sales order.

Important fields:
- VBELN: Sales Document Number.
- AUART: Sales Document Type.
- KUNNR: Sold-to Party.
- VKORG: Sales Organization.
- VTWEG: Distribution Channel.
- SPART: Division.
- AUDAT: Document Date.
- VDATU: Requested Delivery Date.
- BSTNK: Customer Purchase Order Number.
- NETWR: Net Value of the Sales Order.
- WAERK: Document Currency.

5. VBAP Table

VBAP contains the details of each sales order item.

Important fields:
- VBELN: Sales Document Number.
- POSNR: Sales Document Item.
- MATNR: Material Number.
- ARKTX: Item Description.
- KWMENG: Order Quantity.
- VRKME: Sales Unit.
- NETWR: Net Value of the Item.
- ABGRU: Reason for Rejection.

6. VBEP Table - Schedule Lines

Each sales order item has one or more schedule lines. A schedule line splits the item quantity by delivery date.

Important fields:
- VBELN: Sales Document Number.
- POSNR: Sales Document Item.
- ETENR: Schedule Line Number.
- EDATU: Schedule Line Date.
- WMENG: Requested Quantity.
- BMENG: Confirmed Quantity.

The requested delivery date is the date asked by the customer. The confirmed delivery date is the date the company can commit to after the availability check.

7. Sales Area

Every sales order is assigned to a sales area, the combination of three organizational units:

- Sales Organization: the unit responsible for selling and for the conditions of sale.
- Distribution Channel: the way products reach the customer, for example direct sales or wholesale.
- Division: a product line or group of products.

8. Partner Functions

The business partners of a sales order are identified by partner functions. The internal codes come from German terms:

- AG (Sold-to Party): the customer who places the order.
- WE (Ship-to Party): the customer who receives the goods.
- RE (Bill-to Party): the customer who receives the invoice.
- RG (Payer): the customer who pays the invoice.

One customer can hold several partner functions in the same order.

9. Sales Order Statuses

In SAP S/4HANA, the status fields are stored directly in the header (VBAK) and item (VBAP) tables. The former status tables VBUK and VBUP are no longer used.

Status values:
- A: Not yet processed (not delivered, open).
- B: Partially processed (partially delivered, in process).
- C: Completely processed (fully delivered, completed).
- Blank: Not relevant.

Main statuses:
- Overall Processing Status (GBSTK): progress of the whole sales document.
- Overall Delivery Status (LFSTK): delivery progress of the order.
- Item Delivery Status (LFSTA): delivery progress of a single item.

10. Delivery and Billing Blocks

A block stops the next step of the process until it is removed:

- Delivery Block (LIFSK): prevents the creation of the outbound delivery, for example during a credit check.
- Billing Block (FAKSK): prevents the creation of the billing document, for example while prices are verified.

11. Late Sales Orders

A sales order is considered late when its requested delivery date is in the past and its delivery status is still not delivered (A) or partially delivered (B).

12. Follow-on Documents

- LIKP / LIPS: outbound delivery header and items.
- VBRK / VBRP: billing document header and items.
- VBFA: sales document flow, which links the sales order to its deliveries and billing documents.

13. Sales Order API in SAP S/4HANA Cloud

In SAP S/4HANA Cloud, sales orders are exposed by the API_SALESORDER OData V4 service (Sales Order A2X).

Main entities:
- SalesOrder: header data, for example SalesOrder, SalesOrderType, SoldToParty, RequestedDeliveryDate, OverallDeliveryStatus.
- SalesOrderItem: item data, for example Product, RequestedQuantity, NetAmount, DeliveryStatus.
- SalesOrderScheduleLine: schedule lines, for example ConfirmedDeliveryDate and open confirmed quantity.
- SalesOrderPartner: partner functions, for example the sold-to party.
