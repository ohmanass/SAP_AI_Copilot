
SAP MM - PURCHASE ORDER MANAGEMENT

1. Overview

SAP Materials Management (MM) is a module used to manage procurement processes, purchasing activities, inventory management and material valuation.

2. Purchase Order Structure

A purchase order is a formal document sent to a supplier to request materials or services.

In SAP, purchase orders are represented by two main database tables:

- EKKO: stores purchase order header information.
- EKPO: stores purchase order item information.

3. EKKO Table

EKKO contains general information about a purchase order.

Important fields:
- EBELN: Purchase Order Number.
- BUKRS: Company Code.
- LIFNR: Supplier Number.
- BEDAT: Purchasing Document Date.
- EKORG: Purchasing Organization.
- EKGRP: Purchasing Group.

4. EKPO Table

EKPO contains the details of each purchase order item.

Important fields:
- EBELN: Purchase Order Number.
- EBELP: Purchase Order Item Number.
- MATNR: Material Number.
- WERKS: Plant.
- MENGE: Order Quantity.
- NETPR: Net Price.

5. Purchase Order Lifecycle

The purchasing process generally follows these steps:

1. Create a purchase requisition.
2. Create a purchase order.
3. Send the purchase order to the supplier.
4. Receive the materials.
5. Post the goods receipt.
6. Process the supplier invoice.

6. Goods Receipt

A goods receipt records the arrival of materials at a plant.

In SAP, goods movements are represented in material document data. In modern SAP S/4HANA, MATDOC is the central table for material document data.
